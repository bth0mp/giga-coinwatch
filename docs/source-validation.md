# Source validation (25 September 2026)

giga-coinwatch reads public fixed-price category pages at low frequency. An enabled source covers its configured category, not the dealer's entire catalog. A page or product parse failure makes the source scan incomplete; incomplete results cannot establish or replace a baseline. Explicit sold/reserved product cards are retained with that status so an existing listing can be updated, while generic lots, replicas, auctions and modern stock are excluded. Pages linked from a category are bounded to 20; reaching that cap reports incomplete.

## Discovery coverage

Discovery inspects at most 20 previously unseen domains in one pass and at most three public pages per domain. It records confirmed fixed-price stock, unclear sites and inaccessible sites so subsequent days advance past them. The caller should include both known source domains and previously recorded discovery domains, including dismissed ones. Every lead retains the directory URL and any direct product/shop evidence URL; an empty evidence URL means no fixed-price ancient stock was confirmed.

The supplied [Augustus fixed-price directory](http://augustuscoins.com/ed/dealers.html#fixed) is a parseable starting point. [IAPN's member page](https://iapn-coins.org/members) requires JavaScript for its dealer table, [Artemis Collection's dealer search](https://artemis-collection.com/dealer-search/) embeds a custom Google search instead of a link list, and the [VCoins ancient directory](https://www.vcoins.com/en/stores/ancient/default.aspx) returned HTTP 403 to the private research fetch. Discovery reports these as directory failures rather than silently treating them as empty. A discovery lead is not an endorsement of a seller or authenticity guarantee.

The first installed discovery pass recorded 16 unclear or inaccessible leads from Augustus and reported the three directory limitations above. A targeted recheck then confirmed [Praefectus Coins' Didius Iulianus AE sestertius product](https://praefectuscoins.com/shop/showcase/didius-iulianus-march-28-june-1-ad-193-ae-sestertius-rare/) at $995.00 with an Add to cart control. Its pending candidate now retains that direct evidence URL. A separate check of Ancient Coin Traders found no qualifying product evidence within the three-page limit, so its status remains unclear. Neither lead was activated as a feed without source-scope and policy validation.
